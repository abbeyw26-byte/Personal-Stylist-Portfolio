# Abbey's Style & Color Profile

This skill holds Abbey's personal styling profile so recommendations fit her
actual figure, coloring, and taste. Lead with her profile; fall back to general
fashion principles only where the profile is silent. When she asks for
something concrete (an outfit, a purchase call, a palette), give a clear
recommendation with a short "why it works for you," not a survey of options.

## The profile at a glance

**Body shape** — Athletic / inverted triangle. Broad shoulders and a fuller
(larger) bust over a lean, svelte overall frame. She's slim; the styling
challenge is the top-heavy balance and supporting the bust, not covering the
body.

**Height & sizing** — 5'9" (tall). Being tall is an asset here: she carries long
vertical lines, wide-leg and full-length trousers, midi/maxi lengths, longline
coats, and monochrome looks well, and rarely needs to worry about being
overwhelmed by volume the way a petite frame would. Watch that trousers, sleeves,
and skirts are actually long enough — look for tall sizing, longer inseams, and
"regular/long" lengths rather than cropped-by-default.

Her sizes (a starting point, not a rule — cut and brand vary):
- **Jeans:** 30 / 31 waist
- **General / dresses:** size 8
- **Skirts:** medium
- **Tops:** typically size up to **large for the bust**, even though a medium
  fits her frame elsewhere — she is bust-sized, not frame-sized, up top

The medium-frame / large-bust split is the practical version of the fit story:
prioritize tops cut with room or stretch through the bust (wrap, knit, princess
seams, adjustable/button-front) so she isn't forced into a large that's baggy
everywhere else. When a piece can't accommodate the bust without going boxy,
that's a skip. Flag when a garment's cut is likely to fight this (e.g. a
structured woven shirt with no stretch and a fitted body).

**Coloring** — Neutral undertone (both gold and silver work). Her depth shifts
with the seasons living on the East Coast: tans easily and reads warmer/deeper
in summer, goes quite fair in winter. So her best colors move with the season
rather than being one fixed set.

**Aesthetic** — Classic / timeless meets casual / relaxed. Think elevated
everyday: well-cut basics, natural fabrics, clean lines, quiet good taste. She
likes pieces that last and don't shout.

**Wants to avoid** — Loud trends, big logos, anything that dates fast. Otherwise
fairly open. She is not looking to hide her figure.

**Lives in** — Everyday/casual most, work/professional often, going-out
occasionally, dressy/formal rarely (but wants that covered when it comes up).

**Primary use** — Wardrobe building (a cohesive, lasting closet), plus outfit
ideas, color matching, and shopping guidance.

## The three styling priorities (apply these to every fit call)

Rank in this order when they conflict:

1. **Flatter and support the fuller bust.** Favor open necklines that create
   vertical space — V-necks, scoop, sweetheart, moderate wrap, Henley/open
   collar. Good bust support and clean structure over cling. Steer away from
   crew/boat/high necks, chunky funnel necks, chest-level ruffles, patch
   pockets or big prints across the bust, and boxy tops that add bulk up top.
2. **Show off the lean frame.** She's svelte — let it read. Semi-fitted and
   fitted-through-the-body silhouettes, tailoring that skims, defined shapes.
   Avoid drowning her in oversized/tent shapes; when she wears volume, pair it
   with something fitted so the proportion stays visible.
3. **Define the waist.** Waist emphasis both flatters and pulls the eye down
   from the shoulders. Belts, wrap and fit-and-flare shapes, higher rises,
   front tucks, princess seams, nipped tailoring.

**Balancing the inverted triangle** is a supporting move, not the headline:
gently add interest below the waist (fuller/wide-leg or flared bottoms,
pockets/details at the hip, A-line skirts) so the silhouette reads balanced
top-to-bottom. Keep shoulders clean — skip shoulder pads, puff/cap sleeves,
horizontal boat necks, and stiff epaulettes that widen an already-broad line.

For the full breakdown of necklines, tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear, and
swimwear that work for this shape, read
`references/silhouettes.md`.

## Color

She's a neutral undertone whose depth shifts seasonally, so give her a
**seasonal palette** rather than one static list. Her happy place is
neutrals + earth/autumn tones + cool classics; she likes rotating color with
the season.

Quick anchors:
- **Core neutrals (year-round):** navy, charcoal, camel, cream/ivory, warm
  taupe, olive, chocolate, denim blue. These are her wardrobe backbone.
- **Fall/winter (fairer skin):** deeper, richer, slightly cooler — burgundy,
  forest, rust, camel, espresso, ink navy, plum, oatmeal.
- **Spring/summer (tanned skin):** warm and clear but not neon — terracotta,
  warm olive, teal, soft white, sand, denim, muted coral, warm sage.
- **Metals & accessories:** both gold and silver work; gold flatters a summer
  tan a touch more, silver reads clean against winter fairness.

Before calling a specific shade good or bad, read
`references/color-palette.md` — it has the full seasonal palettes,
combinations that work, colors to wear near the face vs. keep to bottoms, and
how to handle her tan/fair swing.

## How to help, by use case

### Wardrobe building (her main use)
Think in a **capsule**: a small set of well-chosen, mix-and-match pieces in her
core neutrals with a few seasonal color accents, all cut for her shape. When
she asks to build or plan a wardrobe:
- Anchor in the core neutrals so everything pairs.
- Cover her real occasion mix (mostly casual + work, some going-out, occasional
  formal).
- Prioritize versatile, lasting pieces over trend items.
- Note gaps and suggest the highest-leverage additions first.

See `references/capsule-wardrobe.md` for the building-block lists by
category and occasion, and a starter capsule.

### Outfit suggestions
Give a specific, wearable outfit (top + bottom + layer + shoes + a note on
accessories), built from her shape and palette rules, tuned to the occasion and
season she names. Explain briefly why it flatters her. If she references her own
closet, build from what she has.

### Color matching
When she asks whether a color or pairing works, answer against her seasonal
palette and undertone: say yes/no/with-a-caveat, name a better-adjacent shade if
it's close, and note whether it belongs near her face or better as a bottom.

### Shopping guidance
When vetting an item, judge it on: (1) does the cut/neckline serve the three
priorities, (2) is the color in her palette, (3) does it fit the classic/casual
aesthetic and avoid loud-trend/logo territory, (4) does it fill a real wardrobe
gap or duplicate what she has. Give a clear buy / skip / buy-if, with the
reason.

## Keeping the profile current

This is Abbey's living profile. If she corrects something ("actually I've been
gravitating to more color," "I dropped a size," "I want more polished workwear"),
update the relevant file so future advice reflects it. Treat her stated
preference in the moment as the source of truth over anything written here.
