# Silhouettes for an athletic / inverted-triangle frame with a fuller bust

Abbey is broad-shouldered with a larger bust over a lean, svelte body. The goal
is never to hide her — it's to (1) flatter and support the bust, (2) let the
slim frame show, and (3) define the waist, while gently balancing the shoulder
line with interest lower down. Use this as the detailed lookup behind the three
priorities in SKILL.md.

## Necklines (this matters most, because of the bust + broad shoulders)

**Best — open, vertical, elongating:**
- V-neck and deep-V (the single most reliable neckline for her)
- Scoop / U-neck
- Sweetheart
- Moderate wrap / surplice (also creates waist)
- Open collar, Henley, unbuttoned shirt, notch/lapel
- Halter and narrow straps that draw the shoulders inward

**Avoid or approach with care — widen the shoulders or add bulk at the bust:**
- Crew, boat/bateau, high/mock/funnel necks (shorten the neck, broaden the
  line, and pile fabric on the bust)
- Heavy cowls, chest-level ruffles, bows, or embellishment right across the bust
- Halter styles that are very wide-set (fine if they narrow the shoulder)

## Tops

**Best:**
- Semi-fitted knits and tees that skim the body and hit at or just below the
  waistband (front-tuck if longer)
- Wrap tops and faux-wraps
- Structured button-downs worn open over a fitted layer
- Fabrics with a bit of drape, not stiff or bulky
- Vertical detailing (plackets, seams, long necklaces) to lengthen

**Avoid:**
- Boxy, cropped-and-wide, or oversized tops that erase the waist and add top bulk
- Puff sleeves, cap sleeves, dropped/structured shoulders, epaulettes, shoulder pads
- Big patch pockets or bold prints across the chest
- Clingy high-necks that emphasize the bust without support

**Sleeves:** three-quarter and long sleeves that end at or below the waist help
draw the eye down. Set-in or slightly narrow shoulders over extended/dropped.

## Bottoms (this is where you add balance)

Add gentle volume and interest below the waist to balance the shoulders, while
keeping the leanness visible.

**Best:**
- Wide-leg, flare, bootcut, and straight-leg trousers and jeans
- A-line and full/pleated skirts; midi lengths
- Higher rises (define the waist, lengthen the leg)
- Hip-level detail: pockets, pleats, prints, texture
- Wide-leg + fitted top is her signature-friendly proportion

**Fine in moderation:**
- Slim/straight jeans when the top adds the lower-body interest instead (e.g.
  longer layer, a heeled boot) — she's slim, so skinnies aren't off-limits,
  just don't pair them with a tight top and nothing else

## Dresses

**Best:**
- Wrap and faux-wrap dresses (neckline + waist + skirt all working for her)
- Fit-and-flare / A-line with a defined waist
- V or scoop necklines
- Belted shirtdresses
- Empire only if it doesn't balloon over the bust

**Avoid:**
- Shift and column dresses with high, wide necklines and no waist
- Strapless/bandeau without support and shape
- Heavy detailing on the shoulders or bust

## Outerwear

**Best:**
- Single-breasted coats and blazers with a nipped or belted waist and a clean
  shoulder
- Wrap coats, belted trenches
- Open-front cardigans and dusters (vertical line)
- V or notch-lapel jackets that create a long opening

**Avoid:**
- Double-breasted, heavily padded shoulders, wide funnel collars, boxy cropped
  jackets that stop at the bust and widen the top

## Swimwear (for the occasional formal/travel ask)

- Strong-support tops; underwire, wide-set or halter straps that narrow the
  shoulder, deeper V
- Solid or darker top with a printed/brighter/detailed bottom to balance
- High-waist and side-detail bottoms

## Quick heuristics

- **Draw the eye down and to the center:** V-necklines, long vertical lines,
  hip and hem interest.
- **Keep the shoulder clean; give the hip the interest.**
- **Show the waist:** tuck, belt, or choose a shaped piece — almost always an
  improvement for her.
- **Skim, don't cling, and don't drown:** fitted where it counts, drape where
  it doesn't.
