# Abbey's wardrobe inventory (owned pieces & logged outfits)

A running catalog of pieces Abbey actually owns, plus outfits she's already worn,
so suggestions can be built from her real closet — not just generic ideas. When
she asks for an outfit, prefer pieces logged here and combine them in new ways;
when she logs new pieces, add them; when something leaves her closet, remove it.

Structured as a small knowledge graph: **items** (with attributes + how they fit
her profile) and **outfits** (combinations of items she's worn, referencing item
IDs). Fit notes are read against her athletic/inverted-triangle frame, fuller
bust, neutral undertone, and classic-casual taste.

## Items

### Tops

- **T1 — Black sleeveless tank (fitted knit, racer/side-cut armhole).**
  Body-skimming, scoop-ish neck. Great fit for her: shows the lean frame,
  narrow shoulder line, supports a tucked/high-waist pairing. Core neutral,
  year-round. Very versatile base.
- **T2 — Espresso-brown sheer/mesh scoop-neck cami (thin straps).**
  Going-out piece; scoop neck + narrow straps flatter bust and shoulders.
  Warm brown sits squarely in her palette. Needs the right bra/layer given
  sheerness. Best spring/summer or layered.
- **T3 — Taupe/beige ribbed sleeveless crop (fitted, square/scoop neck).**
  Ribbed knit skims and stretches over the bust (a bust-friendly cut), defines
  the waist when cropped at the high rise. Warm neutral, year-round base.
- **T4 — Rust/maroon tie-front cropped blouse (long full sleeves, deep-V open
  front with tie).** Deep V + vertical tie is very flattering for the bust and
  elongates; balloon sleeve adds soft interest without widening the shoulder.
  Rust is a favorite autumn tone. Reads fall/transitional. A standout piece.
- **T5 — Black square-neck / off-shoulder short-sleeve crop (fitted).**
  Square neck flatters the bust and collarbone; watch the off-shoulder, since a
  wide horizontal line can broaden the shoulders — keep it sitting low and
  narrow, or wear on-shoulder. Core neutral, going-out.

- **B3 — Light/medium-wash denim cutoff shorts (distressed, frayed hem,
  mid–high rise).** Her summer-casual workhorse (worn in several outfits). High

  weather casual.

### Shoes

- **S2 — Tan/brown wedge mules (heeled).** Dresses up shorts/denim for

## Observations & gaps (for wardrobe building)

- Denim cutoffs (B3) are doing a lot of the lower-body work — a second warm-
  weather bottom (a midi skirt or lighter wide-leg trouser) would extend the
  mix and lean more classic.
- Strong on fitted/cropped tops and warm neutrals; light on structured layers
  (a blazer, a trench) and work-appropriate bottoms — the highest-leverage
  additions per the capsule plan.
- Palette skews warm neutrals (black, brown, taupe, cream, rust) — right in her
  lane; a few cool-classic anchors (navy, a crisp shirt) would round it out.
