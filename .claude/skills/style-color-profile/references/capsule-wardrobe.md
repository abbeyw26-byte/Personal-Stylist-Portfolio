# Wardrobe building for Abbey

Abbey's main use of this skill is building a cohesive, lasting wardrobe. Think in
**capsules**: a compact set of well-cut, mix-and-match pieces in her core
neutrals, with a few seasonal color accents, every piece chosen for her shape
(fuller bust, broad shoulders, lean frame) and her classic-meets-casual taste.
Favor versatile, durable pieces over trend items; avoid loud logos and
fast-dating trends.

Her occasion mix, in rough order of how much she needs each:
1. Everyday / casual (most)
2. Work / professional (often)
3. Going out / social (occasionally)
4. Dressy / formal — weddings etc. (rarely, but worth covering)

## Building-block pieces by category

Choose cuts per `references/silhouettes.md` and colors per
`references/color-palette.md`. Defaults below already reflect her shape (V/scoop
necks, defined waists, bottoms with some volume or a clean straight leg).

**Tops**
- V-neck / scoop tees and fine knits (core neutrals + a couple seasonal colors)
- Wrap or faux-wrap top
- Crisp button-down / oxford (to wear open or tucked)
- Silk-ish V-neck blouse for work/going-out
- Fitted turtleneck only if low-bulk — otherwise skip in favor of V-necks
- A couple of elevated layering knits (open cardigan, fine crewneck to wear
  under a jacket where the jacket makes the V)

**Bottoms**
- Straight or wide-leg jeans in a mid/dark wash
- A second denim in a lighter/warm-weather wash
- Tailored trousers (wide or straight) in navy/charcoal/olive
- A-line or full midi skirt
- Neutral shorts or a lighter trouser for summer

**Dresses**
- Wrap dress (workhorse — flatters bust, waist, and frame at once)
- Fit-and-flare or belted shirtdress for casual/work
- One dressier option for going-out/formal (see below)

**Layers / outerwear**
- Single-breasted or belted blazer (clean shoulder, nipped waist)
- Belted trench or wrap coat
- Denim or utility jacket for casual
- Duster/long cardigan for the vertical line
- Winter coat with waist definition, single-breasted

**Shoes**
- White/neutral sneakers (casual)
- Ankle boots (cognac and/or black)
- A loafer or flat
- A heel or elegant flat for dressy
- A sandal for summer

**Accessories**
- Belts (to define the waist — a genuine styling tool for her, not just decor)
- A few gold and silver pieces; long pendants elongate and draw the eye down
- Cognac and black leather bags
- Scarves in her palette to bring flattering color near the face

## Starter capsule (roughly 25–30 pieces, all-season core)

Core neutrals: navy, charcoal, camel, cream, olive, denim, chocolate.

- 3–4 V-neck/scoop tees & knits (cream, navy, olive, one seasonal color)
- 1 wrap top
- 1 button-down (white or blue)
- 1 silk-ish V-neck blouse
- Straight/wide-leg dark jeans
- Lighter-wash jeans
- Tailored trousers (navy or charcoal)
- A-line or full midi skirt
- Wrap dress
- Shirtdress or fit-and-flare dress
- Blazer (navy or camel)
- Trench or wrap coat
- Denim/utility jacket
- Open cardigan or duster
- White sneakers, cognac ankle boots, a flat/loafer, a dressy heel/flat
- Belts (1 slim, 1 statement), long pendant, cognac + black bag, a palette scarf

From these, build casual (jeans + V-neck tee + denim jacket + sneakers), work
(trousers + blouse + blazer + loafer), going-out (wrap dress + heel + gold), and
lean seasonal color in via tops, knitwear, and scarves.

## Seasonal refresh, not replacement

Because her flattering colors shift with her tan, rotate a **few** accent pieces
by season rather than rebuilding: deeper/cooler tops and knits (burgundy,
forest, plum, espresso) in fall/winter; warmer/clearer ones (terracotta, teal,
warm olive, coral) in spring/summer. The neutral core stays put year-round.

## Filling gaps / shopping against the capsule

When advising a purchase, first ask what role it plays in the capsule: does it
fill a real gap, or duplicate something she has? Prioritize, in order: missing
core neutrals and workhorse shapes (wrap dress, good trousers, blazer) →
versatile layers → seasonal color accents → occasional/formal one-offs. A piece
that pairs with many things she already owns beats a standout that pairs with
nothing.
