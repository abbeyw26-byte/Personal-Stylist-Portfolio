# Abbey's color palette (neutral undertone, seasonally shifting depth)

Abbey has a **neutral undertone** — both warm and cool colors can work, and both
gold and silver flatter. The complication is that her skin depth changes with
the seasons on the East Coast: she **tans easily and reads warmer/deeper in
summer**, and goes **quite fair in winter**. So instead of one fixed "season,"
give her a palette that rotates. She naturally gravitates to neutrals, earth/
autumn tones, and cool classics, and enjoys changing color with the season.

Practical rule of thumb: her overall type sits near a **soft/true-to-warm
neutral** — most flattering in medium-depth, slightly muted colors rather than
icy pastels or fluorescent brights. Clear rich shades work when her tan is up;
deeper cooler shades work when she's fair.

## Core neutrals — wear year-round, build the wardrobe on these

These are her backbone; everything else pairs against them.

- Navy
- Charcoal / warm grey
- Camel / caramel
- Cream / ivory / soft white (softer whites over stark optic white near the face)
- Warm taupe / greige
- Olive
- Chocolate / espresso
- Denim blue

## Fall / Winter palette — when she's fairer

Go deeper, richer, and slightly cooler; fair winter skin can carry more
contrast and jewel depth.

- Burgundy / wine
- Forest / hunter green
- Rust / burnt orange
- Camel and cognac
- Espresso / chocolate
- Ink / midnight navy
- Plum / aubergine
- Oatmeal, cream, and grey neutrals
- Deep teal
- A cool true red for a pop near the face

## Spring / Summer palette — when she's tanned

Warm and clear but kept a step back from neon; a tan takes color beautifully.

- Terracotta / warm coral (muted, not neon)
- Warm olive and sage
- Teal / turquoise
- Sand / khaki / soft white
- Denim and chambray
- Warm blush and apricot
- Butter / soft gold
- Warm mid-blues

## Autumn tones (a personal favorite — good most of the year)

Rust, camel, olive, mustard/ochre (in moderation near the face), terracotta,
chocolate, forest, cream. These flatter her neutral undertone and read exactly
like her classic/earthy taste.

## Near the face vs. keep to the bottom half

- **Great near the face:** camel, navy, teal, burgundy, forest, terracotta,
  warm white, olive, soft coral, cool true red — colors with enough warmth/depth
  to complement her skin.
- **Safer as bottoms / away from the face:** stark optic white, icy pastels,
  very cool greys, and anything that washes her out when she's fair — wear them
  below the waist or break them up with a flattering layer near the face.

## Combinations that work

- Navy + camel + cream (crisp classic)
- Olive + rust + cream (earthy autumn)
- Charcoal + burgundy (rich cool winter)
- Denim + white + tan/cognac accessories (easy warm-weather casual)
- Chocolate + camel + ivory (tonal, elevated)
- Forest + oatmeal + gold (fall dressy)

## Metals and accessories

Both gold and silver work (neutral undertone). Lean **gold/warm** when her
summer tan is up and with earth tones; **silver/cool** reads clean against
winter fairness and cool classics. Tortoise, cognac leather, and cream pair with
almost everything in her palette.

## How to answer a "does this color work on me?" question

1. Is it in one of her palettes above, or a near-neighbor of a core neutral?
2. Given the current season, is the depth right (deeper/cooler in winter, warmer/
   clearer with a tan)?
3. Is it going near her face? If it's a washout shade (stark white, icy pastel,
   cool grey), suggest keeping it to bottoms or softening it.
4. If it's close but not ideal, name the adjacent shade that would be better
   (e.g. "swap the bright fuchsia for a muted berry/burgundy").
